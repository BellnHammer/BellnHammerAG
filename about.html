Our Story

Why We Started

Most homeowners have a contractor story — the one who disappeared mid-project, blew through the timeline, or handed over a final bill nobody expected. Bryce and Trayton heard those stories on job after job.

So in November 2024 they started Bell & Hammer with a simple idea: do quality work, communicate constantly, and never surprise the client. Residential remodels, additions, exteriors and outdoor projects across Kitsap County — and commercial buildouts in King and Snohomish County.

Every job is still run by the founders. When you call, you talk to the people doing the work.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Communication

Regular updates, honest timelines and a real person who answers the phone. You'll always know what's happening, what's next and what it costs.

Craft

Square, level, true and clean. We'd rather take an extra hour to get a seam right than leave something we wouldn't put in our own homes.

Accountability

Written estimates, clear scopes and no hidden costs. If something changes, you hear about it from us first — before it's on an invoice.

Respect

Your home or business stays livable. Floors protected, dust controlled, site cleaned at the end of every day.
