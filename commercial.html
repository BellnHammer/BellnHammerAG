Sectors

Spaces We Build Out

Tenant Improvements

Process

How a Commercial Job Runs

01

Walkthrough

We walk the space with you or your PM, review drawings if you have them, and confirm scope, access and schedule constraints.

02

Scope & Bid

A written, line-itemed proposal — no vague allowances. Clear inclusions, exclusions and timeline.

03

Schedule

We lock in start dates around your tenant, business hours and any landlord requirements. COI issued before day one.

04

Build

One point of contact and regular progress updates with photos. Clean, secured site at the end of every shift.

05

Closeout

Walkthrough, punch list, final cleanup and documentation so you can hand over keys with confidence.

FAQ

Commercial Questions

What areas do you cover for commercial work?

Commercial and tenant improvement projects in King and Snohomish County. Our residential work is focused on Kitsap County.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Bell & Hammer is licensed, bonded and insured (WA L&I BELLHH*754BC). We issue a COI naming the owner, landlord or property manager before mobilization.

Do you work after hours or in occupied buildings?

Yes. We regularly phase work and schedule after-hours shifts so tenants and businesses stay operational, and we keep the site clean and secured at the end of each shift.

Do you handle permits?

We pull the building permits for our scopes and coordinate inspections so the paperwork doesn't land on you.

Can you handle multiple units or properties?

Yes — property managers use us for unit turns, repairs and punch lists across portfolios, with consistent pricing and photo documentation for every job.
