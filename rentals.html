Rental Terms

The Fine Print, Up Front

We're not here to bury you in legal small print. Here's exactly what you're signing up for when you rent from us — no surprises, no gotchas.

Requirements

  • Valid ID: driver's license or state ID required at pickup.
  • Security hold: credit card hold or deposit — released when equipment returns clean and undamaged.
  • Age: 18+ to sign the rental agreement. Some heavy equipment may require 21+.
  • Rental agreement: signed at pickup (or in advance — download below).

Rental Periods

  • 4-Hour: for quick jobs; return within 4 hours. Any use past 4 hours automatically bills the full day rate.
  • Daily: 24 hours from pickup time.
  • Weekly: 7 consecutive days.
  • Monthly: 28 consecutive days.
  • Late returns: billed at next tier up (e.g., late daily → billed 4hr overage rate).

Damage & Loss

  • Normal wear: no charge — that's on us.
  • Damage from misuse: repair cost billed to renter.
  • Lost or stolen: full replacement cost.
  • Cleaning fee: applies if returned excessively dirty or full of debris.
  • Fuel: gas units returned with full tank, or refueling fee applies.

Delivery & Pickup

  • Local delivery available for a fee (varies by distance and item size).
  • Pickup point: our Kitsap County location — arranged at reservation.
  • Site access: customer responsible for safe unloading area for heavier equipment.
  • Same-day service when the schedule allows.

FAQ

Common Questions

What do I need to rent equipment?+

A valid driver's license or state-issued ID, a credit or debit card for the security hold, and a signed rental agreement. You can download the agreement above to sign in advance and speed up pickup.

Do you deliver?+

Yes — we offer local delivery in Kitsap County for a fee that varies by distance and equipment size. Call for a delivery quote. Some items (like the mini track loader) are much easier for us to deliver than for you to trailer yourself.

What if I return it late?+

Late returns get billed at the next-tier rate. A late 4-hour rental rolls into a full daily; a late daily rolls into a 2-day rate, and so on. If you know you're going to run over, call us — we'll usually work with you if we don't have it committed to someone else.

Do I need my own insurance?+

Renter is responsible for damage or loss under our standard rental agreement. If you have homeowner's or contractor's insurance, that may cover you — check your policy. Some jobs (especially commercial) may require you to add us as an additional insured; ask when you reserve.

What if the equipment breaks while I'm using it?+

Stop using it and call us right away. If it's a mechanical failure not caused by misuse, we'll swap it out at no charge or refund your rental for the affected time. If it's damaged from misuse, that gets billed under the damage terms above.

Do you rent to homeowners, or only contractors?+

Both. Anyone 18+ with a valid ID and credit card can rent. Most of our rental customers are DIY homeowners tackling a weekend project.

Can you show me how to use it?+

Yes. At pickup we walk through the basics of operation, safety, and shutdown. If you have detailed questions about how a machine performs on your specific project, ask when you reserve and we'll go deeper. We use this same equipment every day — we know what works.

Do you offer weekend rates?+

Yes — a Friday afternoon to Monday morning rental typically counts as a weekend rate (roughly 1.5x daily). Call to confirm timing based on when you can pick up and drop off.
